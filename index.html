Our Apps

Automated Climate Action Maker (ACAM)

A free, privacy-first Chrome browser extension that analyzes your browsing patterns locally on your device and suggests climate-positive actions — signing petitions, finding refurbished alternatives, and making lower-emission food choices. All processing happens 100% on your machine with zero telemetry. No data ever leaves your device.
